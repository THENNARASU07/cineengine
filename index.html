Under the hood

How it works

Every number on this page comes from a trained model or from ratios measured on real films. Nothing is hand-set.

1

Revenue forecast

Gradient boosting on budget, language and release year, constrained so a bigger budget never lowers the forecast. Trained on 1,189 Indian films from Kaggle and the TMDB API.

R² 0.5867.5% within 2×3×5-fold CV
2

RiskBand: calibrated range

Three quantile models plus conformal calibration give an 80% revenue range. The raw band covered 69.9% of films; after calibration it covered 80.2% of held-out films.

80.2% coveragetypical width 6–9×
3

Hit or flop risk

Random forest on budget, year and the story's sentence embedding, trained on 3,087 Hollywood films (hit means revenue of at least 1.5× budget). Treat it as an indicative risk screen.

AUC 0.61catches 79% of flops
4

Genres and similar films

SBERT embeddings feed a genre classifier and a cosine search over the Indian film set. Posters come from TMDB. Match % is plot similarity, not a prediction.

macro AP ≈ 0.54 vs 0.14 chanceSBERT MiniLM
Data
  • TMDB 5000 movies and a Top-1000 Bollywood box-office list (Kaggle)
  • Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam and Kannada films via the TMDB API
  • 38 placeholder-value rows removed during cleaning
  • Amounts converted to ₹ crore with year-wise average rates
Honest limits
  • Only films with reported budget and revenue; flops are under-represented
  • Community-edited data; Tamil has 109 films, Kannada only 29
  • Plot text did not improve revenue accuracy, so the forecast uses budget, language and year
  • Hit/flop model is Hollywood-trained and not validated on Indian films
  • No star, OTT, distribution-scope or act-structure inputs yet: they need labelled data
